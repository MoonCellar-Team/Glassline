# Changelog

## 1.4 — 2026-10-29

- **Touch controls:** added swipe distance, direction-sector and one-direction-per-touch settings to help prevent accidental jumps.
- **Balance:** added a balance meter and smoother correction on beams and wires. Left/right balance controls work in Story, Speedrun and online races.
- **Launcher:** the save-folder path is now always visible.
- **Fixed:** Enter/A starts the selected level directly in Story, Speedrun, Bag Hunt and multiplayer. Level results now focus Next when available.
- **Fixed:** improved wall-run jump timing and climbing onto nearby walls after swinging, without unwanted automatic climbs after wall jumps.
- **Fixed:** the bag counter now has a slightly transparent background and sits below the timer with centered text.
- **Fixed:** letters with descenders are no longer clipped at the bottom.
- **Fixed:** clicking Play in the browser launcher could crash the game.
